Clock in & out here

Scan with your phone camera when you arrive and when you leave
  1. Open your phone camera and point it at the code
  2. Tap your name
  3. Enter your own 4-digit PIN
  4. Scan again when you leave to clock out
Times are taken from the system clock and your location is checked against the workshop. Only clock yourself in — never anyone else. Forgot? Tell the office the same day.
