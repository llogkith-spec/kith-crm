03

Labour

One SRT is one line. Each line picks its own multiplier from its own hours — nothing pools except inside a single service.

Read the SRT out of TechMan
  1. Check the registration at the top of the screen matches the car.
  2. Click Technical Data in the sidebar.
  3. Click HaynesPro (or Autodata — HaynesPro first if both are there).
  4. Click Repair Times.
  5. Drill in: system → area → job. Rear brakes is Body & Chassis → Brakes → Rear → Pads & Discs.
  6. Type the hours in exactly as shown. Never round an SRT to tidy it — rounding 1.9 up to 2.0 takes about £70 off the quote.
